# Admiral Energy Google Business Profile Growth Playbook

Last updated: October 17, 2026

## Purpose

This playbook gives the owner a truthful, repeatable process for using Google Business Profile (GBP) to introduce Admiral Energy to people near Kings Mountain, North Carolina and measure the resulting website visits, calls, and home-backup inquiries.

It is a manual operating guide. It does not authorize an automated profile edit, a production deployment, paid advertising, or publication of an unverified claim.

## Non-negotiable business truth

- Admiral Energy LLC is veteran-owned and based in Kings Mountain, North Carolina.
- The current retail product is the SideKick PowerBank.
- The exact home-backup designation is **Generac Aligned Contractor**.
- Admiral Energy may provide backup-power education, collect preliminary homeowner interest, and begin a standby-generator planning conversation.
- Appropriately licensed professionals must handle required final electrical design, permitting, definitive proposals, and installation work.
- Do not describe Admiral Energy as a Generac authorized dealer, authorized service dealer, electrician, licensed electrical installer, or independent turnkey installer without verified documentation.
- Do not imply that an installation crew, installation partner, or fulfillment arrangement already exists.
- Do not publish a private residential street address, fabricated service location, customer information, review count, project, certification, event, outage statistic, or testimonial.

## 1. Eligibility checkpoint: decide before editing

Google says a business generally must make in-person contact with customers during its stated hours to qualify for a Business Profile. Google also lists online-only businesses and lead-generation businesses as ineligible. Review the current operating facts against Google's [Business eligibility and ownership guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036?hl=en) before creating, claiming, or materially changing a profile.

The owner must answer these questions and retain the answers with the business records:

1. Does Admiral Energy currently make genuine in-person contact with customers during its stated hours?
2. What present activity creates that contact: an in-person consultation, customer-site visit, delivery, or another real activity?
3. Does Admiral Energy receive customers at a staffed, signed commercial location during stated hours?
4. Is the person managing the profile the owner or an authorized representative?

Decision:

- If the answer to question 1 is **no**, do not create or represent an eligible local profile merely to generate leads. Continue with ordinary website SEO until the operating model is eligible.
- If Admiral visits or delivers to customers but does not receive them at its address, use a service-area profile and hide the address.
- If Admiral receives customers at a location, show the address only when it is genuinely staffed, has permanent signage where required, and customers can visit during the listed hours.

Google's [representation guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en) and [service-area guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481?hl=en) state that a service-area business should not display a residential address merely as a storefront. A virtual office is not a workaround.

## 3. Profile identity and description

### Business name

Use the real-world business name. Do not add phrases such as “Kings Mountain Generators,” “best generator installer,” or service keywords unless they are genuinely part of the consistently used business name. Google's [representation guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en) require the profile to match how the business is represented in the real world.

### Recommended owner-reviewed description

> Admiral Energy is a veteran-owned energy-resilience business based in Kings Mountain, North Carolina. We offer SideKick portable personal power and practical education for homeowners exploring backup-power options. As a Generac Aligned Contractor, Admiral Energy helps homeowners begin the standby-generator planning process. Required final electrical design, permitting, proposals, and installation are handled by appropriately licensed professionals. We focus on clear explanations, realistic product limits, and helpful next steps.

Before publishing, confirm every sentence remains current. Do not add a price, promotion, URL, or unsupported superlative. Google explains how to edit the field in [Manage your Business Profile description](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13682007?hl=en); its profile-data guidance says descriptions should explain the business and exclude promotions, prices, and URLs in [Create a bulk upload spreadsheet for Business Profiles](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3370250?hl=en).

### Business attributes

If the verified profile makes the attribute available, the owner may opt in to the **Veteran-owned** identity attribute. Only select attributes that are accurate. Google documents available identity attributes in [Manage your business attributes](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9049526?hl=en).

## 4. Category review: choose for current activity, not future ambition

Google says the primary category should be the most specific available category that describes what the business currently is; categories affect how the profile is matched to searches. Category names and availability can change, so review the live picker rather than copying an old list. See [Manage your business category](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7249669?hl=en).

Use this process:

1. In the verified profile, open **Edit profile → Business category**.
2. Record the current primary and additional categories before changing them.
3. Search the current Google-provided choices for the narrowest category that matches Admiral's present, eligible, customer-facing activity.
4. Read the category as a customer would. If it implies installation, repair, a retail storefront, or a professional license Admiral does not currently have, reject it.
5. Use the fewest additional categories needed to describe real current activities.
6. Save a dated screenshot of the choice outside this public repository and recheck after Google approves the edit.

Category cautions:

| Category shown in the live picker | Decision rule |
|---|---|
| A category accurately describing current energy-equipment sales or in-person backup-power consultation | Consider only if that activity is actually offered and forms the eligibility basis |
| `Electrician` or an electrical-installation category | Do not select without verified licensing and direct service capability |
| Generator installation, repair, or service category | Do not select unless Admiral currently performs that exact activity with proper authorization and licensing |
| Generator shop or other storefront category | Do not select unless customers can actually visit a qualifying staffed storefront and the described inventory/service is real |
| Solar contractor/company category | Do not select based on education, referrals, or future plans |
| Broad marketing, lead-generation, or online-only category | Do not use to work around GBP eligibility |

The designation **Generac Aligned Contractor** belongs in accurate descriptive content; it does not justify selecting a category that claims a license or service Admiral does not have.

## 5. Service-area review

Google permits up to 20 service areas, entered as cities, postal codes, or other supported areas rather than a radius. Google also advises that the overall area generally should not exceed about two hours' driving time from the business base. Review [Manage your service areas](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481?hl=en) before editing.

Start with a small, owner-confirmed list:

- Kings Mountain.
- Specific Cleveland County cities or ZIP codes where Admiral currently conducts the eligible in-person activity.
- Specific Gaston County communities only if they are presently served.
- Other nearby communities only after the owner confirms actual operational coverage.

Do not add every city in a large radius for ranking purposes. Do not display a residential address. Service areas communicate where service is available; they do not create a guarantee of installation availability.

## 6. Services review

Where the profile exposes a services editor, list only services presently offered. Google may show services to people searching locally and allows supported custom services; see [Manage your services](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9455399?hl=en).

Potential owner-reviewed descriptions:

- **Home backup education** — Plain-English guidance about personal, critical-load, and standby backup options.
- **Standby generator planning consultation** — An initial conversation about household priorities and next planning steps.
- **Backup-power needs assessment** — Preliminary information gathering; not a final electrical design or installation proposal.
- **SideKick product guidance** — Help understanding the current portable personal-power product and its limits.

Do not list generator installation, electrical design, permitting, repair, warranty service for Generac equipment, or turnkey project management unless each becomes a verified current service.

## 7. Tracked website links

Use lowercase, stable UTM values. Google Analytics recommends consistent `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign` parameters and explains how they appear in acquisition reporting in [URL builders: Collect campaign data with custom URLs](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10917952?hl=en).

### Required links

Primary GBP website link:

```text
https://admiralenergy.ai/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp_profile
```

GBP home-backup post link:

```text
https://admiralenergy.ai/kings-mountain/home-backup?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp_post
```

Facebook local-content link:

```text
https://admiralenergy.ai/kings-mountain/home-backup?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=kings_mountain_resilience
```

Optional SideKick GBP post link:

```text
https://admiralenergy.ai/sidekick?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp_post&utm_content=sidekick_demo
```

For individual GBP posts, append a short non-personal `utm_content` value such as `ats_basics` or `load_assessment`. Never place a name, email, phone number, address, order identifier, or other personal information in a URL.

After changing the website link, click it from the public profile and verify:

1. The intended page loads without a redirect loop.
2. The UTM parameters reach the landing page.
3. A test inquiry retains first-touch attribution in the private Netlify form record.
4. GA4 reports the expected source, medium, and campaign without lead PII.

## 8. Posts and real images

Google allows updates with text, photos or video, and an action button. Posts are reviewed for policy compliance, and posts more than six months old are generally archived unless a date range applies. See [Create and manage posts](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en) and the [photos, videos, and posts content policy](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7213077?hl=en).

Publishing checklist:

- Use an **Update** post for ordinary education; use Event or Offer only when there is a real event or offer with accurate terms.
- Use a real photo supplied or taken by David: the SideKick, its controls, an educational sketch, the founder at a real workspace, or a real non-customer-sensitive demonstration.
- Do not use a stock image that implies a completed Admiral installation.
- Do not show a customer home, address, electrical panel, license plate, face, or document without permission and privacy review.
- Do not invent a local outage, weather forecast, event, customer, project, testimonial, or response-time promise.
- Keep contact details out of post text; use the profile's verified **Call now** button if appropriate.
- Link educational home-backup posts to the tracked Kings Mountain page.
- Preview the post, click its link, and verify it is live after Google's review.

Google's current image guidance recommends JPG or PNG files, 10 KB–5 MB, at least 250×250 pixels, with 720×720 recommended, in focus, well lit, and without excessive alterations. See [Manage your Business Profile photos and videos](https://support.google.com/business/answer/6103862?hl=en).

## 9. Reviews: ask neutrally and never gate

Ask only people who had a genuine experience with Admiral Energy. Use the owner-generated review link; do not invent one. Google explains how to copy the link or QR code in [Create a Google link or QR code to request reviews](https://support.google.com/business/answer/16816815?hl=en).

Permitted neutral request:

> Thank you for working with Admiral Energy. If you would like to share an honest account of your experience, you can use our Google review link: [OWNER-SUPPLIED LINK]. Feedback of any kind helps us improve.

Rules:

- Do not offer a discount, gift, entry, payment, or other incentive.
- Do not ask specifically for a five-star or positive review.
- Do not ask only satisfied customers while suppressing requests to others.
- Do not ask a customer to mention prescribed words, an employee, or a service they did not receive.
- Do not publish review text on the website without permission and source verification.
- Reply professionally without revealing customer, project, health, address, or order details.

Google explicitly prohibits incentives, selective positive-review solicitation, and pressure in its [prohibited and restricted content policy](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7400114?hl=en). Google's [review best practices](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122?hl=en) recommend genuine, balanced feedback.

| Day | Owner action | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Review profile accuracy, edit status, hours, phone, website link, and any Google alerts | No unreviewed public change or inaccurate field remains |
| Tuesday | Publish one useful educational GBP update from the content plan with a real image and tracked link | Post status is Live and the destination works |
| Wednesday | Review new inquiries privately and move each through `New → Contacted → Qualified → Partner Assessment → Won / Lost / Nurture` | Every new inquiry has an owner and next action; no PII enters analytics |
| Thursday | Request an honest review from eligible recent customers using the owner-supplied link; reply to existing reviews | Requests are neutral, unincentivized, and sent only after a genuine experience |
| Friday | Record GBP and website funnel metrics; note what changed | Weekly row is complete and anomalies have a follow-up |
| Monthly | Export GBP performance, compare search terms and conversions, review categories/service areas, and refresh real photos | Owner signs off on accuracy; no category or area is expanded merely for ranking |

| YYYY-MM-DD |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |

Monthly interpretation:

1. Compare GBP website clicks with GA4 sessions tagged `gbp_profile` and `gbp_post`.
2. Compare tagged sessions with Netlify's successfully captured `home-backup-assessment` submissions.
3. Calculate inquiry conversion rate only when both numerator and denominator are reliable.
4. Review lead quality privately; do not upload lead names or contact details to GA4 or GBP.
5. Record content or profile changes so performance movement has context.
6. Do not claim that a ranking or lead increase was caused by one post without enough evidence.
