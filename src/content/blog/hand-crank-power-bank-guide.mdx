A hand-crank power bank converts manual motion into electrical input. Its purpose is narrow: provide a last-resort way to create some energy when a wall outlet, vehicle charger, and useful sunlight are unavailable.

It is not a practical substitute for wired charging, and it should not be marketed as a quick way to refill an entire power bank.

## What a hand crank is useful for

A crank can add resilience because it does not depend on the grid or the weather. In an emergency, a small amount of generated input may be more useful than no charging option at all.

Realistic uses include:

- creating limited input after other stored energy has been used;
- supporting brief, essential phone use when no better charger is available;
- adding another fallback method to a prepared emergency kit.

The exact result depends on the product, how long and steadily it is operated, conversion losses, the phone, and the phone's current power demand. Without a documented test for a specific model, a responsible seller should not promise a certain number of minutes of calling or a certain battery percentage from a given amount of cranking.

## What it cannot replace

A hand crank does not replace:

- charging the power bank by USB before an outage;
- a charged second battery pack;
- a vehicle charger used safely outdoors;
- a larger battery system for appliances or medical equipment;
- a communication and evacuation plan.

Ready.gov recommends keeping electronics charged before an outage and planning alternative power sources for devices in its [power outage guidance](https://www.ready.gov/power-outages).

## A better charging priority

Use the fastest, most dependable option available:

1. Charge by wired USB before severe weather or travel.
2. Conserve the stored battery for communication and essential tasks.
3. Use a compatible vehicle or another charged battery when safe and available.
4. Treat a small solar panel as supplemental input in useful sunlight.
5. Reserve the hand crank for last-resort input.

Always follow the instructions supplied with the device. Stop using any battery product that is damaged, swollen, leaking, unusually hot, or otherwise behaving abnormally.
